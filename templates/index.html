Our Roots & Heritage

Building Trust, Supplying Quality

In The Name of ALLAH by the grace and blessings of Almighty,Our story started in 1964 with late Mohamed Ibrahim Sahib and late Haji Shiek Abdul Kader who founded Sathik Hardwares in Redhills for Agricultural Products, Hardware & Paints. Growing with our customers, we opened Sathik Traders in 2003 for Pumps and Plumbing materials, followed in 2024 by Sathik Enterprises for Bath and Sanitaryware. Today, you get complete solutions under one roof.

For over 60 years, Sathik Groups has been providing traditional services and authentic products to the families and builders with genuine Hardware, Plumbing, and Building materials. Sourced directly from authorized manufacturers, we stand by your side to build your dream home.

For every family, building a home is a big dream made with hard work and love. Since 1964, we have honored that trust with honest retail prices, 100% genuine products, and friendly expert guidance for every step of your build.
